# Business Operations

The Business Operations domain covers the day-to-day processes and workflows that keep an organization running — procurement, invoicing, compliance checks, and operational reporting. A Business Operations Specialist needs visibility into process health, clear documentation of standard operating procedures, and tooling to track exceptions and bottlenecks across cross-functional workflows.

## Key Concerns

- Process documentation and standard operating procedure management
- Cross-functional workflow visibility and exception tracking
- Operational reporting and KPI monitoring
- Compliance and audit trail maintenance
