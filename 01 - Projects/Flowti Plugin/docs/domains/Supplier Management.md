# Supplier Management

The Supplier Management domain encompasses the lifecycle of external supplier relationships — onboarding, contract management, performance evaluation, and risk assessment. It intersects with Business Operations for procurement workflows and with Data Governance for supplier data quality and compliance.

## Key Concerns

- Supplier onboarding and qualification workflows
- Contract lifecycle management and renewal tracking
- Supplier performance scoring and review cadence
- Risk assessment and compliance documentation
