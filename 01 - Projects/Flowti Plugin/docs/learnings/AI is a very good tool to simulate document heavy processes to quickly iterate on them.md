The amount of documentation needed to make software applications transparent, traceable, and audit-able makes it hard to dry-run such processes as the generated artifacts from code are usually scattered around various tools, the vast amount of different programming-languages and environments makes it near impossible to have somewhat realistic data. 

By combining best-practices from requirements-engineering, product-management, and quality-management it's possible to have dedicated agents running, simulating needed roles in this process and produce acceptable output to understand friction and information-depth in the whole process.
