# Chain Mail

Interlocked iron rings forming a flexible coat of armor. Heavy to wear all day but Marcus doesn't complain — it's saved him twice that he knows of.

## Details

- **Type**: Equipment — body armor (heavy)
- **Slot**: Body
- **Found**: Marcus (worn on duty); guardhouse armory
- **Defense**: High — significant protection against slashing and piercing
- **Weight**: Heavy; movement speed slightly reduced when equipped
