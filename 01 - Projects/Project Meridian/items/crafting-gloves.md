# Crafting Gloves

Thick fingerless leather gloves, permanently stained with red and brown dye. Sable wears them for every job that involves hide manipulation or dye application.

## Details

- **Type**: Equipment — hand protection (artisan)
- **Slot**: Hands
- **Found**: Sable (worn in workshop)
- **Defense**: Light — protects against dye burns and awl slips
- **Use**: Required for leatherworking actions; reduces crafting failure chance
