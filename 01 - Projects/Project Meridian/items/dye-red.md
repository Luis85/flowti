# Red Dye

A small ceramic pot of deep crimson leather dye, sourced from crushed beetles and set with alum. Sable uses it sparingly — it's expensive to restock.

## Details

- **Type**: Inventory item — crafting material (dye)
- **Found**: Sable's workshop (craft quarter)
- **Use**: Required ingredient when crafting red-colored leather goods
- **Stock**: Limited quantity; triggers restocking behavior when low
