# Leather Scraps

Offcuts and trimmed edges from hide processing. Sable generates these as a byproduct and keeps a bin of them for small patch-work and practice pieces.

## Details

- **Type**: Inventory item — raw crafting material
- **Found**: Sable's workshop (craft quarter)
- **Use**: Ingredient in leatherworking recipes; can be sold cheaply in bulk
- **Source**: Produced automatically when Sable crafts larger goods
