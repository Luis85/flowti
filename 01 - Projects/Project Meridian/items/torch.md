# Torch

A pitch-soaked wooden torch that burns steadily for several hours. Marcus keeps one on his belt during night patrols — the gate can go dark in an instant.

## Details

- **Type**: Inventory item — light source
- **Found**: Marcus (carried during patrol); guardhouse stores
- **Use**: Illuminates dark areas; required for night patrol behavior
- **Duration**: Burns for one patrol cycle before needing replacement
