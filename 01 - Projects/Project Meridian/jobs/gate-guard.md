# Gate Guard

Watches the town entrance, controlling who enters and exits. Patrols the perimeter at intervals and responds to disturbances — the first line of defense against trouble.

## Details

- **Type**: Job
- **Location**: Town gate; patrol route around the perimeter
- **Primary Actions**: Stand watch, patrol route, inspect travelers, respond to alerts
- **Needs Profile**: High physical demand; rest required after long patrol shifts
- **Agent**: Marcus
