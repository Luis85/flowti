# Leatherworker

Crafts leather goods from raw hides — belts, satchels, armor pieces, and custom orders. Works with dyes, awls, and patterns to produce goods sold locally or via the market.

## Details

- **Type**: Job
- **Location**: Craft quarter workshop
- **Primary Actions**: Process hides, cut and stitch leather goods, apply dye, fulfill orders
- **Needs Profile**: Moderate physical demand; requires sustained focus during precision work
- **Agent**: Sable
