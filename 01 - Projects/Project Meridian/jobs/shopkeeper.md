# Shopkeeper

Runs a market stall, buying and selling goods throughout the day. Skilled at reading customers, managing inventory, and setting prices that keep both parties happy.

## Details

- **Type**: Job
- **Location**: Market square
- **Primary Actions**: Restock stall, sell goods to customers, buy from suppliers, manage inventory
- **Needs Profile**: High social interaction; regular hunger/thirst breaks between transactions
- **Agent**: Elena
