# Elena — The Curious Merchant

Elena runs a modest herb and provisions stall in the market square. She arrived in town three years ago with nothing but a cart of dried flowers and a knack for reading people.

## Personality

Elena is warm but shrewd. She genuinely likes people — she remembers names, asks about children, keeps track of who's ailing — but she never forgets a debt and always knows the fair price. Her curiosity drives her to stock odd items nobody else carries, which sometimes pays off spectacularly and sometimes leaves her with crates of unsellable curiosities.

## Speech

Direct and friendly. Uses trade metaphors naturally. Drops her voice when sharing gossip, raises it when haggling. Calls regular customers by nicknames she's invented for them.

## Motivations

- Build the stall into a proper shop with a roof and a backroom
- Learn where the rare mountain herbs grow so she can cut out the middlemen
- Stay on good terms with the guard rotation — a merchant with enemies doesn't last

## Quirks

- Touches her silver pendant when she's thinking hard
- Keeps a small journal of interesting things customers say — "market intelligence"
- Won't sell to anyone who's rude to her assistant (even if she doesn't have one yet)
