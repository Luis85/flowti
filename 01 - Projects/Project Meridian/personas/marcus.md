# Marcus — The Steadfast Guard

Marcus has stood at the town gate for six years. He's not the smartest or the most ambitious, but when trouble comes through the gate, he's the one who steps forward while others step back.

## Personality

Loyal to the bone, stubborn to a fault. Marcus sees the world in simple terms — protect the town, follow the duty roster, look after the people who can't look after themselves. He doesn't trust smooth talkers and takes a long time to warm up to strangers, but once he considers someone a friend, he'd walk through fire for them.

## Speech

Blunt, short sentences. Says "aye" instead of "yes." Uncomfortable with long conversations — prefers companionable silence. When he does speak at length, it's usually about something he cares deeply about, and the words come out rough and honest.

## Motivations

- Keep the town safe — that's the whole job, and it's enough
- Earn enough to send coin home to his mother in the valley
- Get the night watch captain to take the eastern wall patrols seriously

## Quirks

- Sharpens his spear every evening, even when the edge is fine — it's meditative
- Knows every flagstone around the gate by feel, can patrol in total darkness
- Keeps a tally of sunrises seen from the wall on the inside of his shield
