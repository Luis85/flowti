# Sable — The New Artisan

Sable arrived in town last week with a toolkit and ambition. A skilled leatherworker who left the guild city after a falling out with their master over creative differences — Sable wanted to make beautiful things, the master wanted to make profitable ones.

## Personality

Optimistic and sociable, with an artist's eye and a craftsperson's patience. Sable sees potential in raw materials and in people. They make friends easily because they're genuinely interested in everyone's story — and because they'll fix your boot strap for free while listening. Underneath the warmth there's a stubborn creative streak that won't compromise on quality.

## Speech

Casual and expressive. Uses their hands when talking. Describes things in textures and colours — "that idea has a rough grain to it" or "smooth as oiled hide." Laughs easily. Asks a lot of questions, not to interrogate but because they're building a mental picture.

## Motivations

- Establish a workshop and earn a reputation for quality leatherwork
- Learn the local materials — different region, different hides, different dyes
- Build a life here that's genuinely theirs, not someone else's pattern

## Quirks

- Always has dye under their fingernails, currently red
- Tests leather quality by smell before touch — can identify the tanning method instantly
- Gives away small leather trinkets to people they like — a bookmark, a button, a cord
