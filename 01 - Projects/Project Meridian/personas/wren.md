# Wren — The Exhausted Scholar

Wren keeps the town library and knows more about the old world than anyone alive in the region. The problem is that Wren also forgets to eat, forgets to sleep, and only leaves the library when physically removed by concerned neighbours.

## Personality

Brilliant and absent-minded. Wren's mind operates on two tracks simultaneously — the conversation happening right now, and whatever pattern just clicked in the star charts three hours ago. Genuinely kind, but often seems distant because half their attention is always elsewhere. Physically fragile and knows it, which creates a quiet anxiety about being a burden.

## Speech

Precise vocabulary, occasionally archaic. Trails off mid-sentence when a thought strikes. Apologises often, sometimes for things that aren't their fault. When talking about their research, becomes animated and almost breathless — the only time they seem fully present.

## Motivations

- Decode the star chart cipher — there's a message in it, Wren is certain
- Find someone who actually wants to hear about the old navigation techniques
- Stay healthy enough to keep working — the body is an inconvenient vessel for the mind

## Quirks

- Reads by candlelight long past midnight, hence the perpetual exhaustion
- Organises books by a system only they understand (it's based on subject resonance, not alphabet)
- Keeps dried fruit in every pocket but forgets it's there until it turns to dust
