# Library Desk 2

The second reading desk from the window in the main hall of the town library — the one with the best light and the most surface area. Wren claimed it years ago and no one has disputed this.

## Details

- **Type**: Property — designated workspace
- **Location**: Town library, main hall
- **Owner/Occupant**: Wren
- **Features**: Large surface, inkwell holder, built-in lamp bracket, adjacent shelf access
- **Use**: Wren's primary research station; star chart and current notes are always present here
