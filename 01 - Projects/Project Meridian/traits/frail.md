# Frail

A body that demands more than it gives. Frail agents tire quickly and feel hunger faster than their peers — they compensate with planning, not stamina.

## Game Effect

- **System**: NeedsDecaySystem
- **Modifier**: hungerDecayScale = 1.5
- **Effect**: Hunger need decays 50% faster than baseline
- **BT Influence**: Threshold for "seek food" behavior triggers earlier; rest behaviors have elevated priority
