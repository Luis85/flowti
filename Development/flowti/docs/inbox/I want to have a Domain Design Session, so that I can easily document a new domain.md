I want to decompose a domain into it's parts.

A domain consists of at least Services, Events, and Flows.

Every Entity can relate to Actors, while editing a Domain Entity we always need to be able to add Actors to the Entity.

I want to decompose a flow into it's steps of tasks and attach Actors, Requirements, User Stories, and Notes to it.

I want to decompose every unit related to this domain, to further deepen my documentation. I also want to decompose up, down, left, and right from the single Entity.
