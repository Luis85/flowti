# Meeting Transcription and Action Items

Meetings generate decisions and action items that often get lost without immediate documentation. Auto-transcription would capture meeting audio, generate a structured vault note with summary, key decisions, and action items, and link it to the relevant session or project. This reduces the manual effort of post-meeting documentation and ensures nothing falls through the cracks.
